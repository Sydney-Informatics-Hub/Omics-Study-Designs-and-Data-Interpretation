# Foundations of Omics Study Design, Bias, and Data Interpretation ​

### Your experiment is only as good as its design.

Too often, bioinformatics teams receive omics datasets only after it’s too late to fix the real problems, batch effects tangled with biology, sample sizes too small to support the hypothesis, or crucial metadata missing entirely. Once the data has been generated, the options become limited, expensive, and sometimes impossible.

This workshop brings the conversation upstream, where decisions have the greatest impact.
You will learn how to design omics experiments that are statistically sound and analysis ready before a single sample is processed, and how to critically assess datasets for bias, quality issues, and common interpretation errors that can mislead even experienced researchers.
